Find files matching a glob pattern (fnmatch-style). `pattern` is required and must be a non-empty relative pattern; `path` (default `.`) scopes the search; `page` (default 1) selects a slice. Wraps results in `<search_result tool="glob" total=".." shown=".." page=".." next_page=".." pattern=".." scope="..">…paths…</search_result>`. Unscoped recursive globs (`**/...` at root) and over-broad listings are refused with a `<search_result … hint="…">` empty envelope — read the `hint` and narrow the scope. Bracket-glob syntax is permissive: a malformed bracket matches nothing without erroring.
